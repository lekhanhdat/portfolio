## Overview

An Industrial IoT / MES web platform for factory production monitoring: OEE, output, cameras, equipment alarms and product data. I was the main frontend contributor.

## My contributions

- Built OEE and production views with charts, cards and real-time device timelines.
- Developed camera live and playback: HLS streaming, multi-camera layouts, a playback timeline and a recording calendar.
- Moved product data to server-side search, sorting, filtering and pagination, with inline editing.
- Built equipment alarm management: severity levels, real-time notifications and actions.
- Developed production-order and machine operation workflows.

## Product scope

Production monitoring, OEE, cameras, equipment alarms, product data and reporting.
