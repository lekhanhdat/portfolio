## Overview

An Industrial IoT / MES web platform for monitoring production in a factory: OEE, production output, cameras, equipment alarms and product data. I was the main frontend contributor.

## My contributions

- Built OEE and production monitoring views: area-level cards and charts, performance cards, product lists and real-time device timelines.
- Developed the camera live and playback module with HLS streaming, multi-camera layouts, a playback timeline with hover time and wheel scrolling, a calendar that marks days with recordings, and clip navigation.
- Moved product master data from client-side processing to server-side search, sorting, filtering and pagination, with inline create, edit and duplicate, autocomplete, and immediate refresh after edits.
- Built equipment alarm management: severity levels, real-time notifications, navigation to the affected equipment, and acknowledge, clear and close actions.
- Developed production-order and machine workflows: start and stop commands, device timelines and operational status.
- Added the area monitoring page and area cards on the dashboard, and moved the app from top navigation to side navigation.

## Product scope

The product includes a dashboard, area monitoring, alarms, cameras, product details, reports and settings.

## Engineering highlights

- Server state with TanStack Query: caching, refetching, request cancellation and payload mapping.
- Product data searched, sorted, filtered and paginated on the server instead of in the client.
- Responsive layouts across desktop, tablet and mobile, with loading, error and disabled states.
